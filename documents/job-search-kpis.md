# Job Search KPI Framework

## The Funnel

```
Activity (you control) → Responses → Screens → Interviews → Offers
```

## Weekly Targets

| Activity | Target/Week | Why This Number |
|---|---|---|
| Applications sent | 5 | Quality over volume — tailored apps only |
| Cold outreach sent | 5 | Emails/LinkedIn to hiring managers |
| Follow-ups sent | 3 | Nothing dies from one touch |
| LinkedIn posts published | 3 | Builds inbound pipeline |
| Networking conversations | 2 | Calls, coffees, events |

## Leading Indicators (Check Weekly)

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Pivot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application → Screen rate | >15% | 10-15% | <10% → Resume needs rework |
| Outreach → Response rate | >20% | 10-20% | <10% → Messaging needs rework |
| Screen → Interview rate | >40% | 25-40% | <25% → Storytelling needs rework |
| Interview → Offer rate | >25% | 15-25% | <15% → Interview prep needed |
| LinkedIn post → inbound msg | >1/week | 1/2 weeks | <1/month → Content strategy failing |
| Portfolio visits/week | Growing | Flat | Declining → SEO/distribution problem |
| Resume downloads/week | >3 | 1-3 | 0 → Portfolio not converting |

## When to Pivot

**After 4 weeks with <10% app→screen rate:**
- Resume variants aren't landing. A/B test different lead stories.
- You may be targeting wrong-level roles (too senior/junior).

**After 4 weeks with <10% outreach→response rate:**
- Cold emails aren't compelling. Test shorter emails, different hooks.
- Try product-led intros (Variant C) instead of credential-led.

**After 2 failed interview loops:**
- Story bank needs sharpening. Record yourself, listen back.
- You may be underselling the AI builder angle.

**After 6 weeks with zero inbound from LinkedIn:**
- Content isn't reaching the right people. Shift to commenting on hiring managers' posts.
- Tag specific people. Engage before you post.
