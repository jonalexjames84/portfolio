## 6. Content Strategy (30-Day Plan)

### Posting Cadence
- **3 posts per week** (Tue/Wed/Thu, 8-9am PT)
- Mix: 2 insight/framework posts + 1 "here's what I built" post per week
- Engage in comments on 5 AI/PM posts daily for 10 minutes (this matters more than most people think for algorithmic reach)

### Post Ideas

**Week 1**

**Post 1 — Hot Take: "PMs who can't prompt are the new PMs who can't read a SQL query"**
Share the insight from Berkeley ProductCon that interviewers are now asking "what's in your prompt library?" Frame it as: this isn't optional anymore. List 2-3 frameworks (RISE, RACE) with one-line descriptions. End with "What's in yours?"

**Post 2 — Here's What I Built: "I built a full product ecosystem with no engineering team"**
Pottery Friends story. Lead with the numbers: 150 beta members, 15 repos, 59 migrations, solo build. The hook: "AI didn't replace my engineering team. I never had one." Show 2-3 screenshots. End with what you learned about AI-native development.

**Post 3 — Framework: "The one question I ask before prioritizing anything"**
"What's the fastest path to learning something we don't know?" Walk through a real example from Pottery Friends where you killed the marketplace feature after user interviews. Discovery and delivery aren't separate phases.

**Week 2**

**Post 4 — Conference Takeaway: "The 3 most useful things I learned at Berkeley ProductCon"**
Cherry-pick from your Aakash Gupta blog post. Focus on the copilot stack (Claude Projects + Cluely + NotebookLM), the prompt library interview question, and managing AI agents. Link to your full writeup.

**Post 5 — Hot Take: "Managing AI agents is a PM skill, not an engineering skill"**
Distill your blog post on this topic. PMs already manage ambiguity, set constraints, and evaluate outputs. Agent management is product management with a different interface.

**Post 6 — Here's What I Built: "I built a CRM from scratch instead of paying for Mailchimp"**
Cluck GTM story. Why you built a custom CRM for an indie game studio. The decision framework: when does build-vs-buy flip? Show the admin panel. End with the cost savings.

**Week 3**

**Post 7 — Insight: "The home screen redesign that doubled engagement"**
Pottery Friends case study. You shipped a feed-first home screen. Data said users skipped it and went straight to events. You killed your own feature and rebuilt around reality. The lesson: your hypothesis is not your product.

**Post 8 — Framework: "How I evaluate whether to use AI for a PM task"**
Build a simple 2x2 (speed gain vs. quality risk). Some tasks AI accelerates (drafting PRDs, analyzing user feedback, prototyping). Some tasks it degrades (stakeholder conversations, hard prioritization calls, team motivation). Be specific with examples.

**Week 4**

**Post 9 — Here's What I Built: "From co-founding a game studio to building investor materials with AI"**
Frame Story story. LLC formation, 5-year financial model, SAFE structure, GTM pipeline. The unglamorous work of zero-to-one. Show one slide from the investor deck or a screenshot of the financial model.

**Post 10 — Reflection: "15 years of shipping products, here's what actually matters"**
Career retrospective. Zynga to building solo with AI. The thread: understanding what it costs to build makes you a better PM. End with what you're looking for next — this is your "open to work" post without saying "open to work."

### Engagement Multipliers
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours for the first 24 hours after posting
- Tag 1-2 relevant people per post (speakers you've seen, PMs whose work you admire) — but only when genuinely relevant
- Repost your blog articles as LinkedIn articles (not just links) for better algorithmic treatment
- Join and actively comment in: Lenny's Newsletter community, AI Product Managers group, Product School group
