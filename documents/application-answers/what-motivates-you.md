I dropped out of school, which turned me into someone who never stops learning. Nobody handed me a credential, so I've had to earn every room I've been in.

When I left Zynga, I realized how hard I was going to have to work to keep up. That pressure became the thing I'm most grateful for, because it made me someone who connects deeply with the people around me. I needed mentors, collaborators, and honest feedback more than most people did. That turned into a habit: build real relationships, solve problems together, share in the wins and the failures. The best work I've done has always come from teams where people genuinely had each other's backs.

What motivates me every day is connection and results. Connection means the people, clients, colleagues, anyone I'm in the trenches with. Results means knowledge, growth, and progress. Not just shipping something, but coming out of it sharper than when I went in.
