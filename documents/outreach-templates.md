# Outreach Templates for PM Job Search

All templates use these placeholders:
- `[COMPANY]` -- target company name
- `[PRODUCT]` -- specific product or feature
- `[PERSON NAME]` -- recipient's name
- `[SPECIFIC DETAIL]` -- something concrete about the company, product, or person
- `[ROLE]` -- job title
- `[MUTUAL CONTACT]` -- shared connection name

---

### When the Role Does Not Fit (Redirect Gracefully)

**Subject: Re: [their subject line]**

[PERSON NAME],

Appreciate you thinking of me. This particular role is not quite the right fit -- [brief, specific reason: e.g., "I'm focused on AI/ML and dev tools rather than ad tech," or "I'm targeting IC senior PM roles rather than people management right now"].

That said, I would love to stay on your radar. What I am looking for: senior PM at a seed-to-Series B company in AI/ML, dev tools, or consumer platforms. Bay Area or remote. Product-led culture where PMs own outcomes.

If something closer to that crosses your desk, I am easy to reach. Portfolio: jonmartin.pro

Thanks,
Jon

### When Asked About Salary Expectations

[PERSON NAME],

Happy to talk comp. I want to be straightforward so we do not waste each other's time.

I am calibrating to market rate for a senior PM with 15 years of experience, including founding PM roles and hands-on technical building. I am open on the exact structure -- base, equity, and the stage of the company all factor in. A strong equity package at a Series A means something different than a higher base at a Series C.

What I care about more than a specific number: the role has real ownership, the team ships fast, and product is a first-class function, not a support role.

Can you share the range the company has budgeted? That is usually the fastest way to figure out if we are aligned.

Jon
