# Career Plan

## Level Preference (rank 1-3)

1. Senior PM at AI/ML company or AI-forward startup (Series A-B)
2. Senior PM at growth-stage SaaS / dev tools / consumer platform startup
3. Lead/Director PM at a mid-stage company where product drives growth

## Industry Preference

AI/ML first, SaaS and dev tools second, consumer platforms third. Open to B2B or consumer. Strong preference for companies building AI products with real users, not pure research labs. Gaming experience is deep but not the target — looking to apply PM skills in the AI market.

## Function / Role Type

Product Manager, ideally 0-to-1 or growth. Technical PM roles are a strong fit given builder background. Not interested in pure platform/infra unless at a top AI company. Open to founding PM roles at early-stage startups.

## Remote vs In-Person

Bay Area based (Concord, CA). Open to hybrid or in-person in SF/Oakland/East Bay. Remote acceptable for the right role.

## Target Companies Preference

Seed-to-Series B startups in AI/ML, SaaS, dev tools, or consumer platforms where product drives growth. Companies where PM owns the outcome, the team ships weekly, and technical depth matters. Top-tier picks: Anthropic, companies building AI-native products, developer tools companies. Open to larger companies if the PM culture is strong and the product is compelling.

## Addressing-Weaknesses Analysis

### Weakness 1: Gaming-heavy background, targeting AI/tech roles
**How to address:** Lead with Pottery Friends and Swob — both are AI-native development projects outside gaming. Highlight Claude Code as a daily tool, not a buzzword. The portfolio site, mobile app, and prototypes are tangible proof. Blog post on AI agent management shows active thinking in the space. Frame gaming as "shipped at scale in high-velocity environments" — the skills transfer, the domain doesn't limit.

### Weakness 2: No FAANG/Big Tech PM experience
**How to address:** Lead with metrics and impact, not company prestige. $50M revenue, 6M users, 10M+ installs. Frame startup experience as end-to-end ownership that big-tech PMs don't get: I did product, design, analytics, GTM, and stakeholder management, often as the only PM. First PM at Genies (85 people) and solo builder at Pottery Friends demonstrate ability to operate independently at high velocity.

### Weakness 3: Laid off in Nov 2024, career gap since then
**How to address:** The gap IS the story. Co-founded a game studio (Frame Story), built a product solo (Pottery Friends, 150 beta members), started consulting (Swob). This isn't a gap — it's the most productive period of the career. Frame it as: "I used the time to build, not just search." The AI-native development work is directly relevant to the roles being targeted.

## Dream Offer

**Company:** Anthropic or comparable AI-native company
**Role:** Senior PM, developer tools or AI products
**Comp target:** $350K-$400K total comp (base + equity + bonus). For Series A-B startups, $280K-$380K TC is realistic. Anthropic-tier companies: $400K-$650K TC per levels.fyi (3/2026).
**Why this specifically:** Building AI products with AI tools is the intersection of 15 years of PM experience and the last year of hands-on building. Want to be at the company defining how AI tools work, not just using them.
**What would make you say no:** Pure research role with no shipping. IC-only with no path to broader influence. Below-market comp.
